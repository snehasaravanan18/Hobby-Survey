Product Requirements Document: Student Hobby Survey 

Overview 

Build a web-based survey for undergraduate students to collect information about their hobbies and lifestyle habits. The app includes a survey form and a results page displaying aggregated data. Responses are stored in Supabase. 

 

Tech Stack 

Framework: Vite + React 

Database: Supabase (PostgreSQL) 

Hosting: Azure Static Web Apps 

Charting: Recharts
